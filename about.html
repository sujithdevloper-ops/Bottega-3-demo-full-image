01 — ABOUT BOTTEGA

Architecture as a dialogue between land, material, climate, and human ritual.

Established in 1995 in Calicut, Bottega is a practice of architects, structural thinkers, and master stonemasons dedicated to climate-responsive regional architecture across South India. We create enduring structures that age gracefully alongside the landscapes they inhabit.

Our methodology merges traditional South Indian vernacular craft—laterite masonry, reclaimed teak joinery, hand-chiseled granite, and terracotta roof sculping—with contemporary spatial engineering. We approach every site by conducting a granular analysis of its microclimate, natural wind corridors, sun trajectories, and indigenous vegetation.

Rather than imposing external architectural statements, Bottega buildings emerge from the soil, utilizing passive cooling courtyards, rain-sheltering verandas, and thermal stone masses. Over three decades and 120+ realized projects, our practice has expanded to include master planning eco-resorts, luxury tropical estates, and custom furniture labs—always honoring the tactile relationship between human ritual and natural elements.
