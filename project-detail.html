House 1

Residential Architecture

Typology Residential
Built-up Area 6,400 sq.ft
Year 2024
Principal Architect Ar. Nixon Thomas

Conceived as a seamless architectural continuum between natural terrain and domestic rituals, the layout unfolds around a central open-sky courtyard—a natural thermodynamic lung that draws in cooling coastal breezes and filters soft, diffused sunlight into the surrounding verandas.

Honoring the craft vernacular of South India, the residence is built from locally quarried laterite stone blocks left naturally exposed to celebrate their earth-red porosity. Reclaimed teak woodcraft frames expansive shaded openings, while deep overhanging eaves shelter the living sanctuaries from tropical monsoon rains.
